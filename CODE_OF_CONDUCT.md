# Kartikay Imaging Toolkit code of conduct

Be respectful, inclusive, and constructive. Harassment, discrimination, threats,
and disclosure of private information are unacceptable. Discuss technical work
in good faith and respect the privacy and boundaries of other contributors.

Kartikay Srivastava is responsible for moderation in this distribution's project
spaces. A private reporting channel must be configured before a community launch.
The original community policy is preserved in the contributor records.
