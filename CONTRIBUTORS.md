# Contributors

Kartikay Imaging Toolkit is a project led and maintained by **Kartikay Srivastava**.
This distribution builds on the Insight Toolkit (ITK), developed by its upstream community.

## Upstream organizations

Credit to the Insight Software Consortium, NumFOCUS, Kitware, and the research,
engineering, and scientific communities that created and maintained ITK.
